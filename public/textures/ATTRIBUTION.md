# Room materials

The following photographed PBR materials are from Poly Haven and are released under CC0:

- **Wood Floor**: https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_floor — 2K color, 1K OpenGL normal and roughness maps.
- **White Plaster 02**: https://polyhaven.com/a/white_plaster_02 — 1K color, OpenGL normal and roughness maps.

License: https://polyhaven.com/license

Assets are served locally. Linen, oak grain, the woven rug and the softly blurred garden view are generated by this application's code; they do not use third-party image services.
